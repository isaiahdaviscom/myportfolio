Premier Design + Build Group is a full-service construction and remodeling firm serving residential and commercial clients across the greater Chicago area. When I came on board, the brand had strong word-of-mouth but virtually no digital marketing infrastructure to convert that reputation into measurable leads online.

## The Challenge

The specific objective: build a cohesive brand identity and digital marketing presence from the ground up within 12 months, targeting a **25% increase in qualified inbound leads** generated through digital channels. A time-bound milestone was set at 6 months to have all primary brand assets and the website live.

## My Role

As **Graphic Designer and Marketing Specialist**, I handled the full brand rollout — logo design, brand standards guide, print collateral, photography direction, and all digital marketing assets. I also managed paid social creative on Meta and designed project case study pages to showcase completed work.

## What We Achieved

- Delivered complete brand identity system — logo, color system, typography, and templates — within **3 months** (6 weeks ahead of milestone)
- Grew organic social following from **0 → 1,200+** followers across platforms within the first year
- Increased inbound digital leads by **32%** year-over-year, exceeding the 25% target
- Produced **8 project case study layouts** that became the primary conversion tool for proposal meetings
- Designed and printed **3 trade show displays** used across 2 regional home-improvement expos
