S&C Electric Company manufactures some of the world's most advanced electrical switchgear and distribution equipment. When I joined the web team in June 2021, their internal tooling portal was a patchwork of legacy markup that hadn't been meaningfully updated in years — slowing down engineers who relied on it daily.

## The Challenge

The goal was specific: modernize the frontend of three internal-facing dashboards within the first six months, reducing average page-load time from roughly 6 seconds to under 2 seconds on the corporate VPN. Achieving a measurable 65% performance improvement within that window was relevant to employees across four facilities who used the tools every shift.

## My Role

As **Frontend Developer**, I owned the component architecture from the ground up. I audited the existing codebase, identified rendering bottlenecks, and introduced a component library built on vanilla JS and CSS custom properties — no framework dependencies to fight IT's security review. I authored accessibility-compliant navigation patterns and ensured WCAG 2.1 AA conformance across all updated views.

## What We Achieved

- Reduced average page-load from **~6 s → 1.7 s** on target dashboards (72% improvement, exceeding the 65% goal)
- Cut uncompressed asset weight by **~41%** through audit and cleanup
- Delivered all three dashboards **on schedule** within the six-month window
- Reduced reported UI-related support tickets by approximately **30%** in the following quarter
- Established a reusable component library that the team continues to extend today
