Accessibility — Color & Contrast

AAA ≥ 7:1 AA ≥ 4.5:1 AA Large ≥ 3:1 Fails Large text = ≥ 24 px regular or ≥ 18.67 px bold

✕ Don't

✓ Do

✕ Don't
